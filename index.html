Lifeboat Network

Privacy Policy

For Hermes, Carla, and Percy: the private Discord bots operated for the Lifeboat Network community.

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hermes, Carla, and Percy (together, "the Bots"), Discord bots developed and operated for the Lifeboat Network Discord server and its closely related servers, collect, use, store, and protect information. These bots are private, purpose-built tools for the Lifeboat Network community and are not made available for use on servers outside of it.

By being in a server where the Bots are active, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

The Bots may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID, username, and display name
  • Server data: server (guild) ID, channel ID, role assignments, and role changes
  • Message content: only when necessary for a specific feature to function, such as automated chat filtering and relaying messages for a support ticket
  • Voice activity: join, leave, and mute/deafen state within voice channels, for moderation logging purposes only
  • Moderation records: case history including warnings, mutes, kicks, bans, and the reasons associated with them
  • Ban appeal submissions: the content of appeals, including your stated ban reason and appeal reason
  • Self-submitted information: such as your birthday (month and day, with year optional), when you choose to provide it through a bot command
  • Volunteer activity data: for members of our volunteer teams, activity and participation data used to administer volunteer recognition and reward programs

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information (such as financial, health, or government identification data), and we do not use any data collected through the Bots to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Operate the core functionality of each bot, including moderation, ticket handling, report processing, and community features
  • Maintain accurate moderation records for accountability and to inform future moderation decisions
  • Respond to reports, appeals, applications, and support requests
  • Administer volunteer recognition and reward programs
  • Investigate and respond to misuse, abuse, or violations of our community rules

How We Store Your Information

Certain information is stored in secure databases, and in the case of some volunteer-facing programs, secure third-party productivity tools. We do not consider the specific infrastructure providers we use to be necessary disclosures for this policy, but all storage systems we use apply encryption to data at rest.

Data Retention

We retain moderation-relevant records, such as case history and ban appeals, indefinitely, as this history is necessary to make informed, consistent moderation decisions over time and to protect our community.

Other information, such as active reports you've made, is retained only for as long as needed to resolve the matter, and is generally deleted or archived once resolved.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction, including encrypting stored data and restricting access to authorized volunteers and staff who need it to perform their duties. However, no security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, in response to valid legal process, or where necessary to protect the safety of our community or comply with Discord's own Terms of Service and policies.

Please note that Discord itself separately collects and processes information under its own Privacy Policy when you use Discord generally, independent of your interactions with our Bots.

Children's Privacy

The Bots are not directed at, and are not intended for use by, children under the age of 13, consistent with Discord's own minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently done so, we will delete that information, except where it forms part of a moderation record we are permitted to retain under the Data Retention section above.

Your Rights and Requesting Deletion

You may request access to or deletion of your data by emailing us at lifeboatdiscordadmin@gmail.com.

Upon request, we will delete the personal data we hold that is not otherwise covered by the exception below. This includes your username, display name, and any self-submitted information such as your birthday.

Exception: where you have an associated moderation record (such as a ban, mute, kick, or warning), we will retain your Discord user ID linked to that record, without your username or display name, to preserve moderation case history, prevent ban evasion, and protect community safety. We will not retain more than the minimum identifier necessary for this purpose.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. When we do, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the Bots after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions or concerns about this policy? Contact us at lifeboatdiscordadmin@gmail.com.
